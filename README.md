# Study Nook
Study Nook is my personal space to organize my day and make room for a little progress. It helps me remember what I need to do, keep track of schoolwork and everyday tasks, and write down notes along the way.

I can plan ahead, look back at previous days, and check off tasks as I finish them. The focus timer helps me time my study sessions and take breaks.

Tasks and notes save automatically in my browser. I can also back up my planner and restore it when moving to another browser or device.
